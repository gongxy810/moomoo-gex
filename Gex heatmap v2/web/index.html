How to read the board

OI-based GEX. For each contract, multiply Gamma × OI × contract multiplier × spot² × 0.01. Calls are signed positive and puts negative under the model assumption. Exposure is in USD per 1% underlying move. OI is a daily clearing figure and does not identify dealer holdings. Heatmap King, Walls and totals cover the selected strike range and expirations. Chart-node strength uses the complete valid strike set for its source, time and expirations before Top N and ≥ King filters are applied together. Strikes is the total number of heatmap rows (20 / 30 / 40 / 50 / 60), balanced around Spot or King.

Velocity. (|current Net GEX| − |baseline Net GEX|) / |baseline Net GEX| × 100. Green means growing magnitude; red means shrinking. Flipped marks a sign reversal. Quotes, Gamma, IV and time to expiry can change GEX even with unchanged daily OI. Missing/incomplete samples stay blank. Baselines precede the target and match contracts, expiries and model. The 1m / 5m / 15m / 30m baseline is the nearest valid capture at or before the target in the same market phase (at most 90 seconds early). A zero baseline stays blank.

Trinity. White marks the closest strike; gold marks King (largest absolute Net GEX). King distance is separate from daily change. Call Wall is maximum Call GEX; Put Wall is most negative Put GEX, within sampled strikes.

GEX by expiration. Each cell keeps its original Net GEX. Color strength is scaled separately for positive and negative values across the displayed strikes and expiration columns: yellow is strongest positive, purple is strongest negative. Outlines and the row above the matrix identify the three largest values on each side within that visible matrix.

Export gex levels. Copies one CSV row for the focused symbol and New York date: symbol, date, Put Wall, Call Wall, fixed Day Expected Move low/high, King. Walls and King use the selected expiration and strike window. Day bounds use the verified prior-close ±1σ range, even if its chart layer is hidden; the moving Live Cone is excluded. A missing or mismatched field disables copying.

Chart. GEX 图 uses TradingView Lightweight Charts to render OpenD OHLC; that library supplies no market data. The separate TradingView mode embeds the official Advanced Chart Widget with FOREXCOM:SPXUSD as a CFD reference. Widget prices do not populate the app's SPX Spot, GEX or Expected Move. Yellow/purple nodes are historical captures. Derived diamonds require paired current prices in the same session. Day Range requires a verified previous-close IV capture and remains fixed. Estimated IV Cone uses current ATM IV and square-root remaining time to the calendar close; Pin freezes its inputs. No synthetic SPX candles or unmeasured accuracy statistics are shown.

Freshness. Price and options timestamps are separate. Poll frequency is not market-data delay. OpenD does not independently timestamp every Greek or OI field. REF forwards use median matched-strike call/put mids, omitting interest rates; they are not cash SPX. Previous closes require unadjusted daily history matched to the OpenD trading calendar. Missing verified closes display —. Successful captures persist locally for 10 days; periods while the program is closed stay empty. Quotes are not uploaded to the private hosted server.
